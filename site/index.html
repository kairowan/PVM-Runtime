业务只写一次，三端原生运行

把业务页面、状态和流程编译成平台绑定的签名字节码，在 Android、iOS 与 HarmonyOS 上交给同一套 VM 验证执行，UI 仍由每个平台的原生控件渲染。

Build protected business experiences once. Keep native rendering, signed delivery, selective legacy migration, and one verifiable runtime contract.
