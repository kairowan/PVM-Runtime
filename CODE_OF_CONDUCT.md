# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

PVM Runtime welcomes contributors and users of every background and experience
level. Participation in this repository, its Issues, pull requests, Discussions,
and project events requires respectful, constructive collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code on their technical merits.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while still protecting users.
- Respect different levels of experience, languages, identities, and viewpoints.
- Keep security reports, private data, and credentials in their proper private
  channels.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and community moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, spam, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not accepted. Technical disagreement is welcome; hostility
toward people is not.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[the repository owner](https://github.com/kairowan). Include links or screenshots
that help establish context. Do not open a public Issue when doing so could expose
a target or sensitive information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, issue a warning, or
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports are reviewed for
context, impact, recurrence, and the safety of affected people. Retaliation
against a reporter is itself a violation.

This policy applies whenever someone represents the PVM Runtime project or
participates in a project-managed space.
